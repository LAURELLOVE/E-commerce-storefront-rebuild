A storefront rebuild focused on one thing: getting to checkout faster.

A headless front end over a lean product catalog — built to demonstrate a fast, distraction-free browse-to-cart flow for a small homeware retailer.

Project brief Meridian Goods is a fictional homeware brand created for this portfolio. The goal: rebuild a slow, template-based storefront as a fast, minimal front end — static product grid, instant cart drawer, no page reloads — the kind of rebuild a small retailer asks for when their existing site feels heavy.
